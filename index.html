Bio

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Division of Biology and Biological Engineering at Caltech. I received my PhD from MIT after graduate studies at Tsinghua University. I earned my bachelor’s degree from Zhejiang University as a Morningside Scholar.

I am fascinated by microbial ecology. I integrate theoretical, computational, and experimental approaches to address how microbes and the environment continually shape one another across scales.
